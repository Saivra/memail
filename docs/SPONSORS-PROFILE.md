## Short bio

Building Memail: open-source, self-hosted JMAP webmail for Stalwart, with mail,
calendar, contacts and files in one focused workspace.

## Introduction

Memail gives self-hosters a modern place to work with email. It combines a
focused inbox, rich-text composition, keyboard navigation, calendar, contacts
and files, built on the JMAP foundation of Bulwark Webmail.

Sponsorship helps fund interface and accessibility work, security maintenance,
Stalwart compatibility testing, documentation and community bug triage. We
prioritize dependable core mail workflows before adding optional AI features.

Memail is maintained by Saivra as an independent downstream fork. We retain
Bulwark's license and attribution, preserve upstream ancestry and review
upstream changes before bringing them into our releases. Sponsoring Saivra
supports Memail; it does not sponsor or imply endorsement by Bulwark.

Source: https://github.com/Saivra/memail-webmail

## Suggested optional tiers

These are proposals for the owner to choose, not published commitments. Avoid
promising support response times, feature delivery dates, private access or
sponsor logos without an explicit maintenance policy. One-time sponsorships
may be enabled without creating separate rewards.

After GitHub approves the account, set `.github/FUNDING.yml` to
`github: [Saivra]`, verify https://github.com/sponsors/Saivra and update
SPONSORSHIP.md/README funding status. Do not route Memail donations to Bulwark
or an unrelated personal account by default.
